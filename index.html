    Why Clean Water and Handwashing Matters

    Prevents Disease

    Clean water keeps harmful germs away from our bodies

    Keeps Us Strong

    Our bodies need clean water to stay healthy and strong

    Helps Communities

    Clean water helps entire communities stay healthy

    Protects the Environment

    Clean water keeps rivers, plants, and animals alive.

    Boosts Happiness

    Clean water lets us play, cook, and live without worry

    Supports Learning

    Kids who stay healthy from clean water don’t miss school.

    Steps for Handwashing

    7 Steps of Handwashing

    1

    Wet your hands with clean water

    2

    Apply soap to your hands

    3

    Rub palms together

    4

    Rub back of hands

    5

    Clean between fingers

    6

    Clean thumbs and nails

    7

    Rinse with clean water
